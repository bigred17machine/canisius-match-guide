With the ball

Bench and training

Run the game from the bench

First ten minutes

Identify their deepest midfielder and the runner on their right. Test the wide press on one clear cue. Have the bench track who controls the next touch on goal kicks, whether the far-side runner gets free, and whether our wide receiver has an inside pass. If they repeatedly get through or over the first wave with control, hold the middle block.

Level

Keep the block compact. Screen Keena-22, check for the short-then-long goal kick, and release left when Bridgemanhel-21 is high. Retain cover and the player underneath the next ball.

Ahead

Recover the far winger earlier. Protect the edge of the box and leave Pili-9 or a winger as the outlet. Avoid wide free kicks. Check how many players they push forward before moving our line.

Behind

Send one interior midfielder toward the cutback. Let the ball-side fullback support when the ball is secure. Keep the other fullback and Shalamba-3 back. Switch early while their block is still shifting.

Three six-minute training blocks

Press and recover

Rehearse both flanks. Have the opposition switch once and play over the press once. Score the rep on pressure at release, sight of the far runner, and control of the next ball.

Exit and finish

Start with a keeper-to-channel pass and a defender tight to the target. Give the collector a choice to switch early or reset. Finish with the forward across the first center back, the far winger behind the other, and the midfielder at the cutback.

Play through the clearance

Alternate the right corner from Amodeo-10, wide service from Manley-6, and an opposition far-post delivery. Keep the rep alive through the first counter pass.
