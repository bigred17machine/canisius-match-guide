How we play the score

First ten minutes

Find their deepest midfielder and check who runs on their right. Go on the first clear pressing cue. On goal kicks, get under the first duel and call the next ball. If they keep playing through us or over us, drop together and make them play in front of us.

Level

Stay compact. Screen Keena-22 and watch for the long ball after their first short pass. If Bridgemanhel-21 goes high, look left as soon as we win it.

Ahead

Far winger, get back early. Keep someone at the edge of the box and leave Franjoine-7 or a winger up for the outlet. Stay on your feet near the box.

Behind

One midfielder joins the box for the cutback. The fullback on the ball side can go once the pass is secure. Sampson-2 and the other fullback stay. Switch before they get across.
